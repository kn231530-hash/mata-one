## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-tier corporate-tech identity engineered for forward-thinking businesses, digital creators, and modern enterprise teams. It projects hyper-competence, trust, frictionless intelligence, and modern speed. The interface balances executive clarity with magnetic consumer-grade vibrancy.

Drawing inspiration from contemporary flagship subscription systems and mobile-first operating systems, the aesthetic merges **Corporate Modernism** with subtle **Glassmorphism and Vivid Tonal Illumination**. Precision-crafted pure white card surfaces sit atop soft ambient background gradients, grounded by high-contrast deep navy typography. Tactile violet-to-azure gradient pills, verified badges, and luminous icon containers deliver high energy while maintaining uncompromising utility and accessibility.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high-chroma tech primaries balanced against crisp, clinical neutrals:

- **Primary (`#0064E0` - Meta Royal Blue):** Commands primary actions, key active navigation points, verified subscription statuses, and primary CTAs.
- **Secondary (`#6C27FF` - Electric Violet):** Powers premium feature tiers, AI intelligence indicators, and gradient pairings with the primary blue (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0064E0 0%, #6C27FF 100%)`).
- **Tertiary (`#00C389` - Signal Mint):** Designates active connected channels, automated agent health, positive conversions, and live platform sync badges.
- **Neutral (`#0F141C` - Deep Obsidian Navy):** Anchors high-contrast typography, replacing pure black with rich cinematic depth.
- **Supporting Tones:** Canvas background utilizes a pristine cool grey tint (`#F5F7FB`), bordered by subtle translucent hairline dividers (`rgba(15, 20, 28, 0.08)`), with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) dedicated to raised interactive cards and modal sheets.

## Typography

The type system is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric modernism, high x-height, and sculpted legibility across both dense subscription matrices and touch-first mobile cards. 

Headlines command authority through heavy weightings (`800` and `700`) paired with tight, confident tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`). Product tier labels (such as "Core", "Advanced", and "Premium") often feature gradient text fills or bold brand coloring. Body copy retains generous line heights (`1.45` to `1.5`) in neutral slate tones (`#4A5568`) to ensure effortless scannability across multi-tier feature checklists and technical breakdowns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an 8pt dynamic scale adhering to mobile-first constraints that scale up into a structured desktop grid:

- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked cards with fluid horizontal padding (`1rem`). Full-width sticky action bars anchor the viewport base. Multi-tier subscription selectors rely on swipeable horizontal carousels with edge-bleed padding.
- **Desktop & Dashboard (>= 768px):** 12-column adaptive fluid grid with `1.25rem` gutters and a maximum bounded layout container of `1240px`. Tier comparison boards align feature matrices side-by-side with locked-height header cards.
- **Spacing Density:** Interior card spacing follows compact, clean rhythm: `1rem` vertical padding between checklist rows, `0.75rem` between icon badges and feature headlines, and `1.5rem` to `2rem` surrounding primary card bodies.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages crisp card stacking, soft colored atmospheric glows, and refined glassmorphic surfaces:

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Cool canvas tint (`#F5F7FB`) with optional atmospheric mesh gradients using ultra-low opacity Royal Blue (`#0064E00A`) and Electric Violet (`#6C27FF08`) positioned in top-right viewports.
- **Raised Containers (Level 1):** Solid white cards (`#FFFFFF`) framed with ultra-subtle borders (`1px solid rgba(15, 20, 28, 0.06)`) and soft ambient shadows (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 20, 28, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 20, 28, 0.03)`).
- **Active / Featured Cards (Level 2):** Elevated cards (e.g., highlighted subscription tiers) feature a soft 2px gradient perimeter or an ambient aura shadow (`0 16px 36px -6px rgba(0, 100, 224, 0.15)`).
- **Floating Modals & Sheets (Level 3):** Frosted glass headers and overlay sheets with backdrop filter blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), white semi-transparent fills (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), and deep perimeter shadows (`0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 20, 28, 0.18)`).

## Shapes

The design system uses a friendly yet precise **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry inspired by modern mobile OS paradigms:

- **Standard Buttons & Inputs:** Rounded with `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) corners for smooth ergonomics.
- **Full Action Pills:** Primary subscription buttons, active filter chips, and verified status badges leverage full pill radius (`9999px`).
- **Feature Cards & Modal Panels:** Radii range from `1rem` (16px) on mobile cards up to `1.5rem` (24px) on featured plan containers and desktop modular widgets.
- **Category Icon Shields:** Feature indicators (e.g., shield, megaphone, integration nodes) reside inside rounded squircle or organic rounded square containers (`12px` to `16px` radius).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Gradient Action:** Full pill (`rounded-full`), filled with `linear-gradient(135deg, #0064E0 0%, #4D32FF 100%)`. White text, bold weight (`600`), accompanied by soft blue drop shadow on hover.
- **Secondary Ghost / Outlined:** White background, 1.5px border (`rgba(0, 100, 224, 0.2)`), royal blue typography. Transitions to light blue tint (`#0064E00A`) on press.
- **Full-Width Mobile Sheet CTA:** Fixed-height (52px), prominent full-bleed touch targets with continuous gradient sheen.

### Cards & Feature Tiles
- **Pricing & Tier Cards:** Clean white surfaces partitioned into clear semantic sections: plan badge, bold price lockup (`$X.XX/month`), integrated app icon pill array, followed by modular benefit rows.
- **Benefit Step Rows:** Clean horizontal list items featuring rounded gradient number/icon blocks (e.g., violet-to-blue gradient square with white glyph) on the left, paired with bold title and bulleted capability items on the right.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Plan Badge:** Top-anchored pill rendered in solid royal blue or electric violet gradient with all-caps micro-type (`label-sm`).
- **Verified Entity Badges:** Scalloped circular blue badge with centered white checkmark, mimicking social business verification indicators.
- **Channel Pill Badges:** Translucent frosted micro-containers displaying channel icons (e.g., WhatsApp, Instagram, Custom AI Agent) with 50% opacity labels.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Clean white inputs bordered by `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, transitioning to a vibrant `#0064E0` focus ring with a 3px soft blue glow.
- **Tier Accordions / Expandable Rows:** Clean borderless list rows with trailing chevron indicators (`>`), supporting quick expansion of in-depth feature specifications.
- **Checkmarks & Toggles:** Verified checks utilize filled royal-blue circular badges with crisp white check marks instead of plain default tick marks.
